One prompt in, one answer out. **No memory, no tools, no plan.** The model doesn't even know which shop it is talking for.

#### Who decides
The **person**. They read the answer, check it and decide what happens next: copy it into an email, ask again, fix it. The person is the orchestrator, by hand.

#### When to use it
- One-off drafts, summaries and ideas, where a person checks the result anyway.
- Exploring what a model can do before building anything around it.

#### Limits
- Nothing is automated or repeatable: the quality depends on who writes the prompt.
- Each question starts from zero: ask "what is my name?" right after telling it.
- It can't know your private facts (orders, policies), so it either refuses or guesses.

#### Try this
Ask *"Write a short, friendly apology for a late delivery."*: one call is all it needs. Then ask *"What's the status of order 1043?"*: it can't look anything up, so it hands the work back to you.
