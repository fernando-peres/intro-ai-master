# Pricing Tiers

Nimbus has two customer tiers. Prices are per parcel, excluding VAT, for parcels up to 10 kg.

| Service | Standard | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|
| Same-Day Chill | €14.90 | €11.20 |
| Next-Day Chill | €9.40 | €7.10 |
| Frost | €19.80 | €15.60 |

## Standard

No minimum volume. Pay by card or by monthly invoice. Claims are capped at €500 per parcel (see [Returns policy](returns-policy.md)).

## Enterprise

For customers who ship at least **1,500 parcels per month**. Enterprise includes:

- The discounted prices above.
- A 14-day claim window instead of 7.
- Claims capped at €2,500 per parcel.
- A named account manager and a quarterly IAR report.

## Surcharges

- Parcels from 10 kg to 25 kg: +€4.00.
- Island deliveries (Madeira, Azores, Balearics): +€12.00, and Same-Day Chill is not available.
- Frost shipments always route through Seville, so they cost more and take one extra day (see [Company overview](company-overview.md)).
