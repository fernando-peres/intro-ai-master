# Nimbus Logistics — Company Overview

Nimbus Logistics moves temperature-sensitive parcels between small labs, bakeries and florists across the Iberian Peninsula. The company was founded in Porto in 2019 by Ilsa Varga and Tomás Reis, and runs a fleet of 214 electric vans fitted with the in-house **ColdBox** sensor.

## Mission

"Every parcel arrives the way it left." Nimbus measures itself by one number, the **Intact Arrival Rate (IAR)**: the share of parcels delivered within their temperature band and without damage. The 2025 IAR was 99.2 %, and the 2026 target is 99.5 %.

## Offices and hubs

| Site | City | Role | Opened |
|---|---|---|---|
| HQ | Porto | Headquarters, engineering | 2019 |
| Hub North | Braga | Sorting, cold storage | 2021 |
| Hub Centre | Coimbra | Sorting | 2022 |
| Hub South | Seville | Sorting, cold storage | 2024 |

Hub South is the only hub with a −25 °C freezer room, which is why every **Frost** tier shipment routes through Seville (see [Pricing tiers](pricing-tiers.md)).

## Leadership

- **Ilsa Varga** — CEO and co-founder.
- **Tomás Reis** — CTO and co-founder; owns the ColdBox platform.
- **Marta Quintela** — VP Operations; owns the hubs and the [Shipping SLAs](shipping-slas.md).
- **Duarte Lobo** — Head of Security (see [Security policy](security-policy.md)).

## Numbers at a glance

Nimbus has 380 employees, delivered 4.1 million parcels in 2025 and serves 2,900 business customers. Revenue in 2025 was €61 million.
