# Returns and Claims Policy

This policy covers parcels that arrive damaged, outside their temperature band, or not at all. Late parcels that arrive intact get an automatic credit instead (see [Shipping SLAs](shipping-slas.md)).

## Filing a claim

1. Open a claim in the customer portal within **7 days** of the delivery date (14 days for **Enterprise** customers).
2. Attach photos of the parcel and, for spoiled goods, the invoice for the contents.
3. Nimbus Customer Care answers within 2 working days.

## What Nimbus pays

- The shipment price, refunded in full.
- The declared value of the contents, up to **€500 per parcel** on the Standard tier and **€2,500** on the Enterprise tier (see [Pricing tiers](pricing-tiers.md)).
- Goods declared above the cap need the optional *Declared Value Cover*, at 1.2 % of the declared value.

## What Nimbus does not pay

- Damage caused by packaging that does not meet the packing guide.
- Claims filed after the deadline.
- Losses during a force majeure event.

## Returning the ColdBox

Customers must hand the ColdBox back to the next Nimbus driver or drop it at any hub. A sensor not returned within 30 days is billed at €38.
