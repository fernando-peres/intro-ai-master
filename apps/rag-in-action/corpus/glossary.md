# Glossary

Terms used across Nimbus documents.

- **ColdBox** — the reusable temperature and humidity sensor inside every parcel. See [Product catalog](product-catalog.md).
- **Excursion** — more than 10 consecutive minutes outside a service's temperature band. See [Shipping SLAs](shipping-slas.md).
- **IAR (Intact Arrival Rate)** — share of parcels delivered within their temperature band and undamaged. See [Company overview](company-overview.md).
- **Fleetline** — the routing service that assigns parcels to vans.
- **Hub South** — the Seville hub, the only one with a −25 °C freezer room.
- **IC (Incident commander)** — the person who leads an incident response. See [On-call runbook](oncall-runbook.md).
- **Force majeure** — an event outside Nimbus's control (strike, red weather warning) during which SLA credits do not apply.
- **Break-glass** — the tool that grants temporary production access, expiring after 4 hours.
- **Declared Value Cover** — optional insurance for goods above the claim cap, at 1.2 % of declared value.
