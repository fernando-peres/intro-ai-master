07Observability by Example
Interactive notes · for decision makers · unit 07

Complaints are feelings. Traces, costs and scores are evidence.

A made-up bike shop runs an AI support assistant. This week three people complain: it is slow, it gave a wrong warranty answer, and the AI bill doubled. The servers look healthy. Each tab uses one part of observability to find a cause, and the last one fixes them and measures the result.
