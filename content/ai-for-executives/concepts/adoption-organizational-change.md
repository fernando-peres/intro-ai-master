# Adoption and Organizational Change

## In one sentence

AI adoption is the sustained change in behavior, workflow, roles, incentives, and capability through which people use an AI system to achieve better outcomes.

## Executive mental model

```text
Access -> trial -> useful use -> workflow change -> outcome -> sustained value
```

License activation and prompt counts measure activity. They do not prove that work improved or value was realized.

## What changes around AI

- task allocation between people and systems;
- decision rights and accountability;
- review, escalation, and exception handling;
- skills and professional judgment;
- performance measures and incentives;
- staffing, capacity, and job design;
- customer or citizen expectations; and
- trust, transparency, and feedback.

## Adoption principles

- involve frontline users and affected people early;
- design around a real pain point;
- communicate capability and limitations honestly;
- train for verification and escalation, not only tool operation;
- make the safe behavior practical under real workload;
- preserve user control and graceful failure; and
- measure effective use and outcomes by group and workflow.

## Questions to ask

- Whose daily work changes, and what do they gain or lose?
- What incentives support or undermine responsible use?
- Are users over-trusting, under-trusting, or bypassing the system?
- Which expertise might weaken through automation?
- How will released time or capacity be converted into value?
- Can affected people give feedback or challenge outcomes?
