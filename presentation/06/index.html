Objective

Understand what a harness is: the layer around a GenAI model that contextualizes, extends, coordinates, constrains and operationalizes it. Tell apart the three layers of a GenAI system (the model, the harness, and the context and knowledge) and why the quality of the system depends on the harness as much as on the model.

Harness

Harness for GenAI systems capability extensions
