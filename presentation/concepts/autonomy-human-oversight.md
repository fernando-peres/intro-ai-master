# Autonomy and Human Oversight

## In one sentence

Autonomy is the degree to which an AI system selects and performs actions without human intervention; human oversight is the practical ability of people to understand, supervise, intervene, and remain accountable.

## Autonomy spectrum

```text
Generate -> Assist -> Recommend -> Act with approval -> Act within limits -> Broad autonomy
 low                                                                    high
```

Autonomy is a design variable, not a maturity target. The right level depends on impact, reversibility, uncertainty, detection, time sensitivity, and the competence of the human overseer.

## Meaningful human oversight

Oversight is not a button added for compliance. The person must have:

- enough information and time to review;
- relevant authority and competence;
- a clear reason to distrust or challenge the output;
- a practical way to intervene or reverse action; and
- incentives that do not reduce review to rubber-stamping.

## Questions to ask

- What is the most consequential action the system can take?
- Is an error detectable before harm occurs?
- Is the action reversible?
- What uncertainty or risk triggers review?
- Can the assigned person actually exercise oversight at expected volume?
- Who can pause the system immediately?
