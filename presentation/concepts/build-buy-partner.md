# Build, Buy, or Partner

## In one sentence

Build, buy, or partner is a decision about where the institution needs control and differentiation, where it can use a standard service, and where external capability should complement internal ownership.

## Comparison

| Option | Strength | Main trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buy | Faster access to mature capability | Less control and possible dependency |
| Build | Maximum tailoring and ownership | Skills, time, cost, and operating burden |
| Partner | Access to specialist capacity and shared execution | Coordination, knowledge transfer, and accountability boundaries |
| Hybrid | Standard foundation plus institution-specific data, workflow, and controls | Integration and shared-responsibility complexity |

Most enterprise solutions are hybrid: they buy models or platforms and build the context, integrations, workflow, evaluation, and user experience that differentiate the institution.

## Decision criteria

- strategic differentiation;
- time to value;
- internal skills and operating capacity;
- data sensitivity and residency;
- integration and customization;
- transparency and assurance evidence;
- service reliability and support;
- pricing at scale;
- intellectual property;
- portability, concentration, and exit.

## Questions to ask vendors

- Which models and subprocessors are used, and can they change?
- Is our data used for training or retained?
- What evaluations, incidents, limitations, and service levels are disclosed?
- Can permissions, logs, and deletion requirements be enforced?
- How do we export data, prompts, evaluations, and workflows on exit?
